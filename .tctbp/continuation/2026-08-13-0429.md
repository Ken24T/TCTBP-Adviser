## Mistakes & Gotchas

- **Callout click race:** an `onClick` toggle on the info button fought `onFocus` (mousedown→focus opens, click→toggle closes) so clicking instantly closed the popover. Fix: no `onClick` — focus (via click or Tab) opens, blur/mouseleave/Escape close.
- **`formatAge` already includes "Observed":** appending `' · Observed '` produced "· Observed Observed just now". Use `' · ' + formatAge(...)`.
- **Multi-edit corruption:** during the single-action-surface edit, one replacement produced a duplicate `|| journeyPastApply` line and stale identifiers — always grep-verify after multi-replaces.
- **`planIntent` can return null:** tests need a `if (!plan) throw` guard before touching `plan.steps`.
- **Search term gotcha in tests:** searching 'ship' also matches Hotfix (its purpose contains "Ship an emergency…"); use unique terms (e.g. 'promote') for exact-set assertions.
- **Memory-file insert split a line** when appended mid-sentence — use str_replace with full-line anchors.
- **Playwright `run_playwright_code` snippets can't use TS `as` casts** — write plain JS in `evaluate` callbacks.
